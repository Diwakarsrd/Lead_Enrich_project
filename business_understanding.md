# Business Understanding

## What is Caprae's Mission?

Caprae Capital's mission is to transform businesses through strategic initiatives by leveraging AI to unlock new growth opportunities. Unlike traditional private equity firms that heavily rely on financial engineering, Caprae is dedicated to creating lasting value through operational improvements and technological innovation. They focus on empowering businesses post-acquisition through their unique SaaS (Software as a Service) and M&A as a Service models. Their approach treats M&A as a seven-year journey where the greater value creation happens after the acquisition rather than at the time of the deal. By introducing practical AI solutions, they help portfolio companies improve decision-making, streamline operations, and ultimately create sustainable value.

## How is Caprae Changing the ETA Space and Broader PE?

Caprae is revolutionizing the Entrepreneurial Transaction Acceleration (ETA) space and the broader private equity industry through several key innovations:

1. **Operational Focus Over Financial Engineering**: While most PE firms focus primarily on financial metrics and quick returns, Caprae emphasizes operational value creation. They bring a founder/operator first approach to investing, prioritizing long-term growth and sustainable business improvements.

2. **M&A as a Seven-Year Journey**: Caprae redefines the traditional M&A approach by viewing acquisitions as long-term partnerships rather than short-term investments. This perspective allows them to work more closely with portfolio companies to implement lasting changes and improvements.

3. **Technology-Driven Value Creation**: Caprae leverages AI and technology not just as tools but as core components of their value creation strategy. They help portfolio companies embrace and integrate AI solutions to drive efficiency and growth.

4. **Founder/Operator Culture**: Caprae recognizes that culture is paramount to long-term success. They prioritize preserving and enhancing the entrepreneurial spirit of acquired companies rather than imposing rigid corporate structures.

5. **SaaS and M&A as a Service Models**: Their innovative service models provide portfolio companies with access to cutting-edge software solutions and M&A expertise, enabling them to scale more effectively than they could independently.

By focusing on these strategic elements, Caprae is changing the narrative in private equity from one of financial optimization to one of operational transformation and sustainable growth.
